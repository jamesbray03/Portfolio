What I Do

I design, prototype, and deploy end-to-end systems. My proficiencies allow me to operate across the entire engineering workflow:

  • Cross-Disciplinary R&D - building and integrating complex software with physical systems
  • Full-Stack Software & Simulation - working from firmware to UI, building cohesive architectures
  • Embedded Systems & Controls - designing real-time control loops and robust firmware
  • System Optimisation - breaking apart systems to locate and eliminate bottlenecks and improve performance
  • AI automation tools - developing advanced agentic systems to produce excellent products and workflows
