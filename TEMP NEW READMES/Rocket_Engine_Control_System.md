Designing and building the fully functioning DAQ and control system for the UoS rocket test stand with [Project Sunride](https://sunride.space/).

This included selecting and integrating sensors (pressure, temperature and load cells), designing signal conditioning circuits, programming microcontrollers for data acquisition, and then building and wiring the whole system into a rugged enclosure suitable for rocket testing environments.

This was a year long project with several trips to Blastech Buxton where we performed cold flow and hot fire tests of many of our liquid rocket engines.
