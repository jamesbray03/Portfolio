I led the control team in a student competition to win a contract for drone manufacturing with a fictitious company. My primary focus was optimizing flight behavior through a PID controller using BetaFlight.

I also modeled a digital twin of the drone in Fusion 360 to optimize its strength-to-weight ratio using Finite Element Analysis (FEA). The manufacturing process involved creating drone parts through laser cutting or 3D printing, and I handled the soldering of the electronics.

By analyzing black-box telemetry, I was able to iteratively improve the controller, enhancing the overall performance of the drone.

We won for the lightest drone out of 31 groups
