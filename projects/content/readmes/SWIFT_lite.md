Swarm Intellligence for Fire Tracking (Lite Version)

This application is the successor to SWIFT, which I developed during my final year dissertation. I ended up spending a lot more time on this platform than the higher fidelity digital twin platfrom as I needed more performance run larger simulations with more drones, and batch test algorithms for comparison.

This application pulls together various python packages and cutting edge methodologies to meet the criterai for the [XPRIZE competition](https://www.xprize.org/competitions/wildfire), where a drone swarm must autonomously track and map a wildfire in harsh time constraints.

I developed several new methods which significantly helped lower the search time, such as accessing NASA FIRMS sattelite data to bias the drones focus area, and creating adaptive navigation methods which optimise drone sensor usage. This novel IP has allowed me contribute to two academic papers, and assist the team through the stages of the competition.
