In the "Engineering You're Hired" initiative, our team was tasked with leveraging swarm robotics to address real-world challenges. We came first place in our area by devising a solution to enhance the efficiency of litter collection in coastal oceans. By introducing movement to static sea bins and orchestrating them in a swarm, we optimized litter collection along coastlines. We had a week to develop this idea into a presentation to pitch the potential implementation in the real world.
